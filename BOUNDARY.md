# Boundary Statement

## What Is Free

### 1. CharacterNet (Open Dataset & Tools)
**CharacterNet** (this repository) is a free, open-source dataset and tool released under CC BY 4.0[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span).

It provides:
1. **Visual Decomposition (VD):** The constituent visual pieces of each Chinese character, built on the 256 foundational visual building blocks[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span).
2. **Phonetic and tonal metadata:** Pinyin, tone, and primary meaning[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span).
3. **The Free Lookup & Validation Tools:** Open schema and scripts to inspect character pieces, pinyin, tone, and meaning[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span).

CharacterNet is the **input layer**—the raw flat-pack material showing you the pieces[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span).

Perceptual decomposition is not trivial work: the eye’s parsing of a character differs from its historical etymology, and making 256 atoms consistently cover thousands of characters is a real design problem, not a lookup table[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span).

### 2. PRS Chinese Web App (Free Interactive Story Builder)
**PRS Chinese** ([https://www.prschinese.sg/](https://www.prschinese.sg/)), powered by CharacterNet, is where learners craft and save their own sticky stories **completely free of charge**[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span):
- **Zero friction & zero tracking:** No sign-up, no login, and no personal data collection[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span).
- **Local browser storage:** Stories you craft live directly inside your browser page[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span).
- **Portable backup:** Saved stories can be exported for safekeeping and imported back anytime[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span).
