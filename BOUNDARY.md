# Boundary Statement

## What Is Free

### 1. CharacterNet (Open Dataset & Tools)
**CharacterNet** (this repository) is a free, open-source dataset and tool released under CC BY 4.0[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

It provides:
1. **Visual Decomposition (VD):** The constituent visual pieces of each Chinese character, built on the 256 foundational visual building blocks[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
2. **Phonetic and tonal metadata:** Pinyin, tone, and primary meaning[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
3. **The Free Lookup & Validation Tools:** Open schema and scripts to inspect character pieces, pinyin, tone, and meaning[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

CharacterNet is the **input layer**—the raw flat-pack material showing you the pieces[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

Perceptual decomposition is not trivial work: the eye’s parsing of a character differs from its historical etymology, and making 256 atoms consistently cover thousands of characters is a real design problem, not a lookup table[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

### 2. PRS Chinese Web App (Free Interactive Story Builder)
**PRS Chinese** ([https://www.prschinese.sg/](https://www.prschinese.sg/)), powered by CharacterNet, is where learners craft and save their own sticky stories **completely free of charge**:
- **Zero friction & zero tracking:** No sign-up, no login, and no personal data collection.
- **Local browser storage:** Stories you craft live directly inside your browser page.
- **Portable backup:** Saved stories can be exported for safekeeping and imported back anytime.

## What Is Commercial

The commercial side of **PRS Chinese** covers physical and packaged products built around the framework:
1. **Books & Print Editions:** Workbooks, illustrated character guides, and physical story/card decks.
2. **Merchandise & Tactile Goods:** Physical learning tools, branded merchandise, and classroom kits.
3. **Custom Commercial Licensing & Partnerships:** Bespoke editions, institutional collaborations, and commercial inquiries.

## Analogy

- **CharacterNet** is the IKEA flat-pack: every piece, labeled and counted, open for anyone to build with[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
- **PRS Chinese (Web)** is the free workbench right in your browser where you snap the pieces together, craft your own sticky story, and take your work home with you—no account required.
- **PRS Chinese (Books & Merch)** are the printed editions, physical card sets, and tactile goods you can hold in your hands.
