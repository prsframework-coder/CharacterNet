# Boundary Statement

## What Is Free
**CharacterNet** (this repository) is a free, open-source dataset released under CC BY 4.0.

It provides:
1. **Visual Decomposition (VD)**: the constituent pieces of each Chinese character (radicals, sub-components)
2. **Phonetic and tonal metadata**: pinyin, tone, primary meaning
3. **Binding constraints**: the raw instruction template that defines the parameters for generating a mnemonic story

CharacterNet is the **input layer**. It is the raw material.

---

## What Is Paid
**PRS Chinese** (www.prschinese.sg) is a commercial platform.

It provides:
1. **Semantic Multiplexing (SM)**: the absurd, finished mnemonic stories generated from the binding constraints
2. **The PRS Engine**: the proprietary AI and storytelling system that transforms visual decomposition into memorable narratives
3. **Curriculum design**: adaptive sequencing, spaced repetition, progress tracking
4. **User experience**: web app, mobile app, parent dashboards, coaching support

---

## Analogy
- **CharacterNet** is the IKEA flat-pack instruction sheet. It tells you what pieces exist and how they should fit together.
- **PRS Chinese** is the assembled furniture, delivered to your home with white-glove service.

Both are valuable. One is free. One is paid.

## Legal Clarity
If you:
- Use CharacterNet for research, training AI models, or non-commercial education → **you are covered by CC BY 4.0. No permission needed.**
- Build a commercial product that generates finished stories from this dataset → **you are free to do so, but the story generation layer (SM) is not provided here.**

The visual decomposition is open. The storytelling engine is proprietary.
