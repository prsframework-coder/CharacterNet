# Boundary Statement

## What Is Free

**CharacterNet** (this repository) is a free, open-source dataset and tool released under CC BY 4.0.

It provides:
1. **Visual Decomposition (VD)**: The constituent pieces of each Chinese character (radicals, sub-components), built on the 256 foundational visual building blocks.
2. **Phonetic and tonal metadata**: Pinyin, tone, primary meaning.
3. **The Free Lookup Tool**: A simple interface where you input a Chinese character, and it displays its visual decomposition, pinyin, tone, and meaning.

CharacterNet is the **input layer**. It is the raw material. It shows you the pieces.

## What Is Paid

**PRS Chinese** (www.prschinese.sg) is a commercial platform.

It provides:
1. **Semantic Multiplexing (SM)**: The absurd, finished mnemonic stories generated from the binding constraints. This includes the “Role in the story” column and the Story Idea generation.
2. **The PRS Engine**: The proprietary AI system that generates tone anchors, enforces the Immutable Story Lock, and ensures the story passes the Charade Test.
3. **Curriculum design**: Adaptive sequencing, spaced repetition, progress tracking.
4. **User experience**: Web app, mobile app, parent dashboards, coaching support.

## Analogy

- **CharacterNet** is the IKEA flat-pack instruction sheet. It tells you what pieces exist and how they fit together.
- **PRS Chinese** is the assembled furniture, delivered to your home with white-glove service.

Both are valuable. One is free. One is paid.

## Legal Clarity

If you:
- Use CharacterNet for research, training AI models, or non-commercial education → **You are covered by CC BY 4.0. No permission needed.**
- Build a commercial product that generates finished stories from this dataset → **You are free to do so, but the story generation layer (SM) is not provided here.**

The visual decomposition is open. The storytelling engine is proprietary.
