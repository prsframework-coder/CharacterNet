What is CharacterNet?

Traditional dictionaries break characters down by historical radicals and etymology. CharacterNet breaks them down into the visual shapes the eye actually perceives.

It is a free, CC BY 4.0 open-source dataset of the 256 foundational atomic building blocks that make up all Chinese writing.

Example: 兔 (Rabbit)
The eye does not see "a historical radical."
The eye sees: A Child (子) + An Opening (口) + Legs (儿) + A Dot (丶).

Why Open Source?

Because visual literacy should be a public utility. Developers, researchers, and educators are free to build upon this dataset to train AI models, build flashcard apps, or create new learning tools.

The visual decomposition is open. The storytelling engine is proprietary.
